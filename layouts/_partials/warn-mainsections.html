{{- /* Warns when params.mainSections names a section the theme has no article template for. The article template is layouts/posts/page.html and the rich feed is layouts/posts/section.rss.xml -- both bound to a section literally named `posts`. That is documented (README.md, docs/config.md: "articles belong in content/posts/"), but mainSections is read by four other templates (404.html, footer.html, home.llms.txt, home.llmsfull.txt), which makes the setting look more general than it is. Point it at content/blog/ and the articles still build -- they just quietly come out with no reading time, word count, dates, sharing buttons, prev/next, related posts, comments or audio, and the section feed loses its full-text content:encoded. This only warns. Changing template resolution here would silently change the output of every site that already works, which is a far worse trade than a line on the console. partialCached with no variant key evaluates once per build; warnidf carries a per-section id so Hugo prints each section once and --ignoreLogs can silence it by name. */ -}} {{- range site.Params.mainSections -}} {{- if not (templates.Exists (printf "%s/page.html" .)) -}} {{- warnidf (printf "mainsections-no-article-template-%s" .) "params.mainSections names %q, but the theme's article template is layouts/posts/page.html. Articles in content/%s/ render without reading time, word count, dates, sharing buttons, prev/next, related posts or comments, and %s/index.xml loses its full-text content:encoded. Either move them to content/posts/ or add layouts/%s/page.html to your site. See docs/config.md." . . . . -}} {{- end -}} {{- end -}}
