"params.mainSections names %q, but the theme's article template is layouts/posts/page.html. Articles in content/%s/ render without reading time, word count, dates, sharing buttons, prev/next, related posts or comments, and %s/index.xml loses its full-text content:encoded. Either move them to content/posts/ or add layouts/%s/page.html to your site. See docs/config.md." . . . . -}} {{- end -}} {{- end -}}
